Before You Start

This app helps rink staff run daily maintenance checks. Please read before continuing:

  1. Your data stays on this device. Rink profile, checklist history, staff names, and photos are stored locally on this device only. Glice does not receive or store this data unless you actively export or send it (for example, by sharing a PDF summary).
  2. Photos. Photos you take are for maintenance records. Avoid including people in photos where possible; your facility is responsible for how these images are stored, shared, and deleted on-site.
  3. Videos. The Video Library streams from Vimeo. Playing a video connects to Vimeo's service, which is outside Glice's control.
  4. No warranty. This app is provided "as is," as a maintenance aid. It does not replace the official Glice manuals, training, or manufacturer instructions, and Glice is not liable for damages arising from its use, to the fullest extent permitted by law.
  5. Your facility's responsibility. Your facility (as employer) is responsible for complying with applicable data protection and labor laws regarding staff names and activity records entered here.
